Where I changed your outline

  1. The ending is now a rewind, not a recovery. Your outline hits the limit, then switches model and continues. On Pro/Max the 5-hour limit is shared across models, so once you hit it, switching to Sonnet won't get you a green check, and a promo that implies it will draws “that's not how it works” comments. The honest and stronger story: scene 5 replays the same session with pace-line on, you see red before the wall, switch, and never hit it.
  2. Pace only recovers with time. Pace is used % minus elapsed %, and used never goes down. So the green check needs a visible time jump. The numbers in scene 5 are real: 58% used at 36% elapsed is ⇡22%; after 1h 40m on Sonnet, 66% used at 69% elapsed is ⇣3%.
  3. The body teaches with a contrast, not a definition. “Is 35% good?” has no answer until you add the clock. Two bars at the same 35% with different “now” markers make that click in one look; the formula comes after, as confirmation.
  4. The 7-day limit gets one callout, no more. It's the same mechanic, and a second story would push the cut past a minute.

Style direction

Recommended · in the cut

Terminal-native

Real Claude Code UI, monospace, the band's own green/red/cyan. Kinetic type only between terminal scenes. Viewers recognize their own tool, so the problem reads as theirs. Cheapest to build and to keep accurate as the UI changes.

Trip computer

Dashboard metaphor: fuel gauge (usage) vs. “range at current speed” (pace). Instantly clear to non-devs, but it takes the viewer out of the terminal and costs more in illustration.

Paceline

Cycling: riders in a line, the leader burns out by going too hard. Fits the name, but it is a second idea viewers must decode in a 40s spot.

Decide before the final cut

  1. Format. I'd ship 16:9 as master and a 9:16 cut for Shorts/Reels/TikTok, with the terminal cropped to the band and prompt. (Default: both.)
  2. Length. 63s now, paced for reading. A 30s social cut drops scene 2 and the 7d callout. (Default: 63s master, 30s cut for Shorts.)
  3. Build tool. Remotion (React, renders MP4 from code, same timeline logic as this page) vs. hand-animating in an editor. (Default: Remotion, committed under mods/pace-line/promo/.)
  4. Voiceover. Captions only, or add your voice? (Default: captions only; your VO is a strong option for a Vietnamese version.)
